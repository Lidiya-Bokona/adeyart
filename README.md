# 🌼 I built something for my little brother

When I was younger I used to draw for him. Years later, he started drawing flowers for New Year and gifting them door to door. So this year, I wanted to give him a New Year’s gift❤️

I built Adey Art a digital garden for his beautiful hand drawn flower🫠 It’s our little digital way of carrying the Enkutatash spirit beyond our neighboorhood, so anyone, anywhere can receive a little piece of Ethiopian New Year through his flowers. Explore, choose your flower and download his drawing to keep or share the Enkutatash spirit with someone you love. Huge thanks to https://github.com/dag-shell for helping me fix some issues i faced and hosting this website on his domain😊
